Warung Mboh Inah

Role: Gameplay Mechanics & Audio Designer

A dark narrative survival horror experience set in a secluded traditional food stall. I developed the player core interaction system, environmental audio atmosphere, and stress-inducing audio cues.

Key Contributions

  • Gameplay Mechanics: Physics-based item grab system for environment props and toggleable flashlight system (on/off).
  • Audio Mechanics: Proximity-based audio volume scaling, occlusion behind walls, and heartbeat acceleration synced to monster distance.
  • Sound Design: Synthesized custom environmental room tones, eerie wind drafts, distorted traditional instruments, and sudden stingers.

Warung Mboh Inah: System Design Specs

1. Item Grab & Flashlight Mechanics

Player interactions built with Unity raycasts for detecting, grabbing, and dropping environmental items, paired with a simple toggleable flashlight system (on/off state control) for navigating dark areas.

2. Dynamic Sanity & Audio Occlusion

Player stress is calculated based on light exposure and entity line-of-sight. The Unity Audio Mixer dynamically routes all footsteps and ambience through a Low-Pass Filter (cutoff frequencies between 450Hz and 20kHz) depending on the player's proximity to hiding closets.

3. Audio Asset Creation

Ambient sound beds were sculpted using pitch-shifted gamelan resonance combined with granular sub-bass rumbles to evoke authentic local folklore dread.
