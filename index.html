Warung Mboh Inah: System Design Specs

1. Flashlight Raycast & Attenuation

The flashlight utilizes a spotlight component linked with an inverse-square intensity falloff curve. Proximity to anomalies invokes a random Perlin noise voltage jitter on the intensity parameter to simulate dying batteries.

2. Dynamic Sanity & Audio Occlusion

Player stress is calculated based on light exposure and entity line-of-sight. The Unity Audio Mixer dynamically routes all footsteps and ambience through a Low-Pass Filter (cutoff frequencies between 450Hz and 20kHz) depending on the player's proximity to hiding closets.

3. Audio Asset Creation

Ambient sound beds were sculpted using pitch-shifted gamelan resonance combined with granular sub-bass rumbles to evoke authentic local folklore dread.
